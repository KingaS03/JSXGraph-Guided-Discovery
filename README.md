# Mathematical Playgrounds with JSXGraph

Interactive mathematical visualizations for guided discovery, experimentation and conceptual understanding.

## About

This repository contains a collection of interactive JSXGraph applets developed for an introductory university mathematics course for economics and social science students at the University of Bern.

The applets are designed primarily to introduce mathematical concepts through exploration rather than to assess previously acquired knowledge. Students can manipulate mathematical objects, vary parameters, investigate examples and counterexamples, develop intuition and formulate conjectures.

Many applets include discovery-guiding questions that support the transition from experimentation and visual observation to formal mathematical understanding.

## Topics

- Limits and continuity
- Derivatives, monotonicity and convexity
- The extreme value theorem
- Newton's method
- Riemann sums and definite and improper integrals
- Taylor polynomials
- Linear regression and optimization
- Consumer and producer surplus
- Profit maximization with 2D and 3D visualizations

## How to use

Open an HTML file in a web browser to explore the corresponding applet. An internet connection is required to load JSXGraph and other external libraries.
