The Extreme Value Theorem

In this applet, you can explore which assumptions guarantee the existence of global extrema and what can happen when one of these assumptions is not satisfied.

Select a function, move the interval endpoints \(a\) and \(b\) directly along the \(x\)-axis, and switch between the closed interval \([a,b]\) and the open interval \((a,b)\).

Explore:

  1. Where are the global minimum and global maximum of \(f(x)=x\) on \([a,b]\)?
  2. What changes for \(f(x)=x\) when the interval is open?
  3. Can the global extrema of \(f(x)=\frac{(x-1)(x+2)(x-2)}{8}\) lie in the interior of the interval?
  4. Find an open interval on which \(f(x)=|x-1|\) nevertheless has a global minimum. Does this contradict the Extreme Value Theorem?
  5. Select the step function and the closed interval \([-1,1]\). Why does the function have no global maximum there, even though the interval is closed and bounded?
  6. State the Extreme Value Theorem in your own words and identify its essential assumptions.
