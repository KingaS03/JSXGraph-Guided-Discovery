Profit Maximisation with a Per-Unit Tax

For each fixed tax value \(t\), the firm chooses the quantity \(Q\). Here, \(Q\) is the decision variable and \(t\) is a parameter: changing \(t\) changes the entire profit function.

Model and Optimum

\[ \pi_t(Q)=-\frac{1}{600}Q^3+(50-t)Q-\frac{1000}{3}, \qquad Q\in[0,300]. \]

For an interior optimum, \(\pi_t'(Q)=0\) gives the quantity \(Q^*(t)=\sqrt{200(50-t)}\). For \(t\ge 50\), however, the constrained maximum occurs at the boundary, where \(Q^*(t)=0\). The corresponding maximal profit is \(V(t)=\pi_t\!\left(Q^*(t)\right)\).

The applet represents these relationships in different coordinate systems. Therefore, always pay attention to which quantities are shown on the axes.
